--- layout: page title: Welcome to I Love E-Poetry lang: en permalink: /en/welcome-to-i-love-e-poetry/ wp_post_id: 11381 translation: "/es/bienvenido-a-i-love-e-poetry/" --- Welcome to I Love E-Poetry! Short-form scholarship on born-digital poetry and poetics. This scholarly blog was launched on December 19, 2011 as a constraint to read and critically reflect upon a work of e-poetry every day, leading me to revisit known works, discover new ones, and expand my knowledge of this emergent poetic genre. Its initial performance was a continuous run of 500 daily entries, completed on May 2, 2013. In support of its mission, it now has an advisory board, a team of collaborators, and new Calls for Postings, as part of its ongoing Phase 2.
