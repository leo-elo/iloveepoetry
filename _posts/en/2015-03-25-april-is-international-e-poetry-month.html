To join the celebrations, the Electronic Literature Organization and I ♥ E-Poetry will be publishing a calendar to highlight e-poetry performance and publication events from around the world. Please use the form below to add events to the calendar, which will be published on April 1, 2015. If you have any questions, please contact Leonardo Flores at leonardo.flores@upr.edu.
