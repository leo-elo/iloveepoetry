La poesía está escondida en las fisuras de la tierra que se deslizan para crear movimiento en las diferentes..." permalink: "/en/2000/tierra-de-extraccion-by-domenico-chiappe-2/" wp_post_id: 13068 translation: "/es/2000-es/tierra-de-extraccion-por-domenico-chiappe/" featured_image: "/assets/images/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/Tierra1.png" work_url: "https://collection.eliterature.org/2/works/chiappe_tierra/antoHome/tierra.html" elmcip_url: "https://elmcip.net/creative-work/tierra-de-extracci%C3%B3n" --- In Tierra de Extracción (1996-2007) (ELC2), Doménico Chiappe's first hypermedial novel, the extraction of meaning is generated through interaction and manipulation. Poetry is hidden in the fissures of the earth that slide to create movement in the different multimedia layers of the work. The novel is composed of 63 hypermedial chapters, each represented by an interactive [key] word. Similar to Hotel Minotauro (2013-2014), Tierra de Extracción is an example of interactive narrative. For example, in one of the chapters (Mangal/Mango Tree), the reader is invited to learn to roll a die interactively in order to unfold the stories hidden behind each of its faces. The interaction with the dice produces an empty mise-en-scène that is fulfilled by aesthetic chance. Rolling the dice becomes the space where chance meets creation. As the reader explores the novel, they create non-renewable reading paths in the aesthetic fissures of the work, leading to a mango tree that tears apart lines of multimedia poetry. The mango tree feeds on memory; it feeds on the blood of those who perished during the Revolution or the construction of Zumaque I, the first major Venezuelan oil well. The oil stains on memory are emphasized with the watercolors that accompany the interactive narrative. Music guides the plot, as there are times when the song tells a different story from the one we read. Let us not forget that not all layers of the work are activated at the same time. There is independence between interactivity and narrativity. As mentioned earlier, sometimes an image, a watercolor, or a song can narrate more than a thousand clicks. Memory games and the intertextuality of images play an important role in Chiappe's work. At times, navigation is guided solely by visual memory and auditory recollection. A selection of photographs and paintings from Menegrande, from periods in the 20th century, underline the richness of the different arts that compose the historical hyperphony of the work. The author uses the metaphorical effect d'amont en aval (ascending and descending) to show the importance of artistic cooperation behind a work of electronic literature.
One of the recurring figures of animation found in the narrative is the ciné-gramme (Saemmer, 2012), which is when a strong relationship between what the text does and what it says is created. This can be observed in the chapter (¡Milagro!/Miracle!) where words fall into the fissures of the Earth, and then rise and evaporate as the narrative voice suggests: "A veces me parece que esta tierra se la ha tragado para que otro la extraiga" (sometimes it seems to me as if this land has swallowed it so that another can extract it).
Chiappe underlines the complex narrative cartography of the work by calling for action and direction from the reader, who by going North, South, East, or West becomes a sailor who decides the pace and development of the plot. However, the navigation and extraction of meaning is not easy; the reader's choice of actions may lead to rich moments of the aesthetics of frustration (Bootz, 2005). As written in one of the chapters of Tierra de Extracción (Autobús/Bus), the reader will learn that... "esta tierra tiene su encanto pero debes descubrirlo" (this land has its magic but you have to discover it)...
