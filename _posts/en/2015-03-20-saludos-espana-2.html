Arantxa Serantes, author of the humanistic blog A duermevela, has recently published an interview about this resource and the potential that digital media represent for poetry. You will notice that the resource is written in English. If you are not fully proficient in English, don't worry — we are translating the resource into Spanish and hope to have it ready by May of this year. In the meantime, if you are interested in learning more about the project and digital literature, visit the excellent Revista 404, in which they conducted an interview with me and translated a series of articles from I ♥ E-Poetry about the poetry of Jim Andrews. We are always here at your service!
