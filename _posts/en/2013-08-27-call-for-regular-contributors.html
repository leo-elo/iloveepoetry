Why write for I ♥ E-Poetry?
  • This is an opportunity to be published as part of a resource with an established and steadily growing international audience.
  • I ♥ E-Poetry is being used in courses, comprehensive exam lists, and is designed for "real-world impact."
  • I ♥ E-Poetry is integrated with the ELMCIP Knowledge Base, and is developing partnerships with the Electronic Poetry Center, the Electronic Literature Directory, and the Consortium of Electronic Literature.
  • Its first 500 entries are being shaped into a manuscript for book/e-book publication by a renown university press. You could be a part of the next manuscript.
Who may apply?
  • Graduate students, graduates with Masters degrees or PhDs in electronic literature, faculty, published writers of electronic literature.
  • People interested in joining a growing team that already includes:
    • an Advisory Board
    • a team of Interns working on an online collaborative lab.
    • and myself as publisher and editor-in-chief.
  • Writers willing to be directed and available to write at least one weekly entry on any genre of electronic literature they're interested in.
Too much of a committment? You may still contribute single (or sequential) entries via the Open CFP, The E-Lit I Love CFP, and future requests for contributions.
