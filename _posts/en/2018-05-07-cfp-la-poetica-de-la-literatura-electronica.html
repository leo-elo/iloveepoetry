Because its initial focus led to the exclusion of works in many of the publications and exhibitions covered, I ♥ E-Poetry seeks entries on these excluded works. While we compile a list of specific works, visit the Reviewed Resources page and search I ♥ E-Poetry to see specific titles and whether they have been reviewed or not. Entries should follow the established guidelines and will be reviewed as described there. The call will remain open until we have reviewed all excluded works. If you are particularly interested in writing about a specific work or group of works, contact me as soon as possible, as I will be assigning some of the new regular contributors to the task.
