La palabra..." permalink: "/en/resources/ejemplos-de-literatura-electronica/" wp_post_id: 12457 translation: "/es/resources/ejemplos-de-literatura-electronica/" featured_image: "/assets/images/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/iloveepoetrysquarelogo.jpg.png" --- Electronic literature has many modalities and genres developed over its more than 65-year history. Here are some examples of works that will show you some of the potential of this type of literature. What is electronic literature? Read a detailed definition in English, or a brief definition in Spanish.

The Word in Motion:

  • Seattle Drift – This poem wants to be freed from the rules of the page that discipline it and force it to be traditional.
  • Bembo's Zoo – Visit a zoo where words transform into animals.
  • Strings – Italic words stretch and turn into other words to form playful poems.
  • Word Crimes – A video full of words that dance to the beat of a parody of a catchy song.

Cinematic Experiences:

  • Bust Down the Doors! – A violent scene described again and again surprises us with its shifts in perspective.
  • Project for Tachistoscope – This hypnotic work narrates the story of a bottomless pit in the middle of the United States.

Interactive Comics:

  • A Duck Has An Adventure – This comic lets you explore many alternate stories in the life and adventures of a duck.
  • Hobo Lobo of Hamelin – This comic presents the story of the Pied Piper of Hamelin, but with a hobo wolf and a psychotic mayor.

Literary Games:

  • The Flat – You have two minutes to discover what happened in this apartment before something terrifying comes for you.
  • Game, Game, Game and again Game – A fun platform game in which you traverse crazy and poetic spaces.

Generated Works:

  • Taroko Gorge – This poem flows like rivers on your screen, recreating the experience of walking through a canyon.
  • Notes on the Voyage of Owl and Girl – Read stories of the maritime adventures of a young girl and her owl companion.
  • White-Faced Bromeliads on 20 Hectares – This poem about a trip to Costa Rica recreates the narrator's shifting mental state.

Artistic Twitter Bots:

  • Glitch [Meta] – The witticisms tweeted by this cat come from comments people make about their cats on Twitter.
  • HaikuD2 – This bot detects tweets that can be cut into the form of a haiku (a poem of 5, 7, 5 syllables) and reveals them as poems.

Multimedia Works:

  • Memory – This fun poem by a Puerto Rican poet is written with memes and about memes.
  • When I Was President – This dictatorial president takes absurd and absurdly effective measures during his short presidency.
  • Nio – This musical and poetic work is a tool for playing with sounds, new letters, and musical phrases.
