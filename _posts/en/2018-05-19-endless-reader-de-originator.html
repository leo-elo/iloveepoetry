Esta aplicación es la continuación de \"Alfabeto sin fin\", que integra palabras reconocibles a la vista con un entorno digital interactivo con..." permalink: "/en/2013/endless-reader-de-originator/" wp_post_id: 14852 translation: "/es/2013-es/endless-reader-de-originator/" featured_image: "/assets/images/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/IMG_2027-300x169.png" work_url: "https://www.originatorkids.com/endless-reader-2/" --- "Endless Reader" is a mobile application for children created by Originator, which has developed other well-known applications such as "Endless Numbers" and "Endless Alphabet". This application is the continuation of "Endless Alphabet," which integrates sight words with an interactive digital environment in order to allow children to hear words broken down into their simplest phonetic segments. For example, in the word "all," the individual sounds of "a" and "l" are shown. By doing this, children can see the function of each of these segments and then see how these segments interact when they combine to form a complete word. The main goal of this application is to provide children with an advantage in their education in an entertaining and colorful way, as can be seen in the introductory sequence below. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=goKbaHzdNY8 When the application is launched, a pack of colorful and funny monsters appears on the screen carrying the title "Endless Reader." They carry individual words or suffixes depending on the meaning of the word itself. What is interesting about the opening is that it gives the application user a preview of the app. The child will be exposed to a series of short words, prepositions, suffixes, and other parts of speech. The monsters will help the child learn to use sight words on their own and in complete sentences.
