This represents an opportunity to discover new and old works, place them in circulation, and enrich the emerging canon of electronic literature. I ♥ E-Poetry seeks entries on these works, a full listing of which will be available soon in the ELMCIP KB (in the meantime, use this list). The entries must follow the established guidelines and will be reviewed as described therein. The call will remain open until we have read all the contents of the ELMCIP Knowledge Base.
