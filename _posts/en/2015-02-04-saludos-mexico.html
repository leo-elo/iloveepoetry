This project was launched on December 20, 2011 as a blog in which I read and reviewed..." permalink: /en/news/saludos-mexico/ wp_post_id: 11715 featured_image: "/assets/images/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/Leo-404-300x300.png" work_url: "http://leonardoflores.net" translation: "/es/news-es/saludos-mexico/" --- I am pleased to warmly welcome you to I ♥ E-Poetry, an academic and encyclopedic resource dedicated to literature created in conversation with electronic and digital technologies. This project was launched on December 20, 2011 as a blog in which I read and reviewed a work of electronic poetry daily — a sometimes epic effort that lasted 500 consecutive days. The project has expanded to become collaborative, opening its doors to receive writings by other experts in various genres of digital literature. Explore its pages to discover more than 600 reviewed works, frequently organized in thematic, technological, or genre-based collections and resources. This resource was developed in English, my area of academic training, but this semester we will be translating I ♥ E-Poetry into Spanish. We hope to have a large part translated before the month of April, when I will visit Mexico City to participate in the event titled "Mañana hoy se ha ido." If you are visiting from Revista 404, published today by the Centro de Cultura Digital in Mexico, you have surely already read my interview or my reviews of Five Pieces of Digital Poetry by Jim Andrews, translations of articles originally published here. If not, you now have some links to learn more. Explore, enjoy, and share. We are here at your service.
