Because its initial focus led to the exclusion of works in many of the publications and exhibitions covered, I ♥ E-Poetry seeks entries on these excluded works. While we compile a list of specific works, visit the Resources Reviewed page and search I ♥ E-Poetry for specific titles to see if they've been reviewed or not. The entries must follow the established guidelines and will be reviewed as described therein. The call will remain open until we've reviewed all the excluded works. If you're particularly interested in writing about a specific work or group of works, contact me as soon as possible, since I'll be assigning some of the new regular contributors to the task.
