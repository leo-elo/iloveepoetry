Why write for I ♥ E-Poetry?
  • This is an opportunity to be published as part of a resource with an established and constantly growing international audience.
  • I ♥ E-Poetry is being used in courses, comprehensive exam lists, and is designed for "real-world impact."
  • I ♥ E-Poetry is integrated with the ELMCIP Knowledge Base, and is developing alliances with the Electronic Poetry Center, the Electronic Literature Directory, and the Consortium of Electronic Literature.
  • Its first 500 entries are being shaped into a manuscript for book/e-book publication by a renowned university press. You could be part of the next manuscript.
Who can apply?
  • Graduate students, graduates with master's degrees or doctorates in electronic literature, professors, and electronic literature writers.
  • People interested in joining a growing team that already includes:
    • an Advisory Board
    • a team of interns working in an online collaboration lab.
    • and myself as editor and editor-in-chief.
  • Writers willing to be directed and available to write at least one entry per week on any genre of electronic literature that interests them.
Too much commitment? You can still contribute individual (or sequential) entries through the Open CFP, The E-Lit I Love CFP, and future calls for contributions.
