This most recent volume published by the Electronic Literature Organization contains 114 works of electronic literature, from 27 countries, and in 13 languages. Read more about this important collection here. I ♥︎ E-Poetry invites the community to submit readings of works of electronic literature published in the ELC3. The guidelines are as follows:.
  • Follow the established submission guidelines.
  • Please make sure the work(s) haven't been reviewed before in I ♥︎ E-Poetry.
  • Contact me with a brief proposal of the work(s) you wish to review.
