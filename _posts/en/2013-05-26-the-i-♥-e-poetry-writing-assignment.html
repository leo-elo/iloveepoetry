If you teach electronic literature, consider having your students write an I ♥ E-Poetry entry.
  • It's relevant:
    • Media-Specific Analysis
    • Software Studies
    • Critical Code studies
    • Close Reading 2.0
    • Authorial Scholarship 2.0
    • Digital Preservation
    • and more...
  • It's brief:
    • 100-500 words (modifiable, of course)
    • Consider series for lengthier entries.
  • It's well described:
    • Anatomy of an I ♥ E-Poetry Entry
    • Submission Guidelines
    • Over 500 examples available
  • It's publishable:
    • Open CFP
    • Focused CFPs
    • Please submit the best ones.
Contact me if you'd like to propose a specific focus for your class to write about-- an e-lit archive, current or past 'zines, a writer or group of writers, genre, methodology, platform, etc. Perhaps we can put together a special "issue" of I ♥ E-Poetry on your topic.
