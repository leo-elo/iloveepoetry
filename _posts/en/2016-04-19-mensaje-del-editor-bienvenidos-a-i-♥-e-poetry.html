What is I ♥ E-Poetry?

This project is designed as an encyclopedic resource on electronic poetry, with concise entries that provide poetic, technological, and theoretical contexts, close readings of the poems, and some strategies for readers to engage with the work. The goal is to broaden the audience for electronic literature beyond the academic world. To extend this potential audience, the blog shares its content through Facebook, Twitter and Pinterest. This resource is written in English, but we will soon be translating it into Spanish.

What is Electronic Literature?

Unlike traditional literature, which is written to be published as ink on paper, electronic literature was designed for digital technologies. The digital word can be programmed to self-generate, move, respond to the reader's actions, change over time, and much more. The example you can see is one of the fun Anipoemas by Ana María Uribe. On I ♥ E-Poetry you can discover more than 675 works, organized into thematic collections and resources, such as children's digital literature, literary bots, and literary video games. Explore, enjoy, and share the riches that I and a team of contributors have reviewed. I invite you to read an interview published in Revista 404 by the Centro de Cultura Digital in Mexico, or my reviews of Five Pieces of Digital Poetry by Jim Andrews, translations of articles originally published here. And if you would like to learn more about my academic work and research, visit my blog: leonardoflores.net.
