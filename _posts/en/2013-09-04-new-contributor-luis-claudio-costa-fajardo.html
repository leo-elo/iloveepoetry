Luís Cláudio Costa Fajardo teaches Design in Digital Media at the Institute of Art and Design, Federal University of Juiz de Fora, Minas Gerais, Brazil. He has a Masters in Technologies of Intelligence and Digital Design (TIDD) from the Pontifícia Universidade Católica of São Paulo. He is currently completing his doctoral dissertation in Literary Studies, investigating the relationship between the Brazilian Post-Concretist poetic movements and poetry in digital media in Portuguese. Luís Cláudio's expertise will be invaluable in enriching our coverage of Portuguese and Brazilian e-poetry. Welcome aboard!
